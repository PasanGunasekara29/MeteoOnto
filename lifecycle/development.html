Mete∞nto Development

How the ontology’s domain model is shaped and extended.

Purpose and scope

Mete∞nto is organized around meteorological observations and the context needed to interpret them: the observed property, result, sensor or platform, place, time, and producing dataset or model.

Its modular structure supports extending the weather model with forecasting, geospatial, extreme weather, and risk concepts.

Competency questions

These questions define the kinds of meteorological knowledge MeteoOnto is designed to represent and connect across its core and domain modules.

#Competency QuestionMain ontology areas
CQ1Which weather observations were recorded at a specific geographic location during a given time interval, and what observable properties and measurements were associated with those observations?Core, Geo
CQ2Which sensors or observation platforms produced observations for a particular location, and what data provider was responsible for those platforms?Core
CQ3Which forecast predictions are available for a specific location and forecast-valid time, and what forecast data and results support those predictions?Forecasting
CQ4Which forecast model and deployment were used to generate a particular forecast, and which platform hosts that deployment?Forecasting, Core
CQ5Which extreme weather events occurred or are forecast to occur in a particular administrative region, and what were their duration, spatial extent, status, and severity?Extreme Events, Geo, Risk
CQ6Which indicators were used to identify or characterize a particular extreme weather event, such as drought, rainfall, temperature, pressure, snow, or wind indicators?Extreme Events
CQ7Which geographic features or critical infrastructure are affected by a particular extreme weather event, and what risk level is assigned to each affected feature?Risk & Safety, Extreme Events, Geo
CQ8Which government authorities are responsible for governing a geographic feature, and what are the corresponding national, state, district, and local authorities?Risk & Safety, Geo
CQ9Which emergency response agencies manage geographic areas affected by extreme weather events, and what types of response organizations are responsible for those areas?Risk & Safety
CQ10Given an extreme weather event and its affected geographic features, which high-risk or very-high-risk geographical features are associated with it?Risk & Safety, Extreme Events, Geo
